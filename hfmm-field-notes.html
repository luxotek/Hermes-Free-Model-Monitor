Free models — what the APIs report, and what is still unresolved

Hermes Free Model Monitor · reference page · built from live Nous Portal, Nous inference catalog and OpenRouter responses

The short answer

There is a pool limit, and it is the number that matters for “heavy use”. OpenRouter reports free_model_daily_requests: {used: 3, limit: 50, remaining: 47} — 50 free requests per day, shared across every free model, not per model. A fallback chain of N free models does not multiply that budget; it splits it.

The best unused signal is quality. OpenRouter reports benchmarks.artificial_analysis (intelligence_index, coding_index, agentic_index) — the only thing that answers “which of these two is actually better”. Only 3 of the 9 free models have it.

Everything else useful is a flag, not a number: provider-side moderation (refusal risk), mandatory vs optional reasoning (wrong tool for aux slots), and whether the free tier is synthesized by Nous rather than offered by the provider.

Open issues to resolve before digging into the data

Pool budget is account-wide. Decide how to surface it — a status-bar readout (47/50 free requests left today) is the cheap option; a per-model projection is not meaningful because the cap is shared.
Quality scores are sparse. 3 of 9 free models only. A Scores column would be mostly empty — decide whether a sparse column is worth it, or whether it belongs in the detail panel.
Mandatory-reasoning models. recursive/flint-v1.0:free and stepfun/step-3.7-flash:free cannot turn reasoning off — poor aux/compaction choices. Needs a visible flag.
Moderation is invisible. cohere/north-mini-code:free is the only moderated free model; that explains otherwise-mysterious refusals. One-character column.
Synthesized free tiers. Only upstage/solar-mini4:free and stepfun/step-3.7-flash:free are Nous-generated free variants (synthesizedFreeVariant, 2 of 398 catalog models) rather than the provider’s own — a longevity signal worth marking.
Free models mostly live outside the Nous catalog. Only 3 of the 9 (longcat-2.5, step-3.7-flash, solar-mini4) appear in the 398-model inference catalog; the rest are enriched from OpenRouter and the portal list. So catalog-only fields are sparse for free models by construction.
Stop looking for per-model rate limits — they do not exist. OR’s rate_limit is marked deprecated. The only real flakiness signal is your own agent.log (429s, fallback fires).
Dead fields. per_request_limits is null in 398 of 398 catalog models, and knowledge_cutoff / frontier are empty for every free model. Do not build columns on them.

Not reported anywhere

  • Per-model rate limits (RPM / TPM) — only the shared 50/day pool exists.
  • Per-model request caps or throttle flags.
  • Latency / throughput — no tokens-per-second in any payload.
  • Deprecation schedule beyond expiration_date (5 models total, 1 free).
  • Live health — nothing says “this model is flaking right now”; only your own logs do.

Decision tree this suggests

Main model

Output ≥ 64k · Released ≥ 6 months ago · reasoning optional · not moderated · highest AA coding / agentic available.

Fallback

Same filters, but weight Released hardest — an older model is far less likely to vanish and leave a dead slot. Keep Output ≥ 32k so it can finish a real task.

Auxiliary (vision / compaction)

Filter on Flags, then prefer the largest Output and oldest Released.

Heavy use

The 50/day pool is shared, so more free models in the chain means more variety, not more volume. Rotate for coverage, not for capacity.
